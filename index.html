ShortsGTAVI Publisher

A personal tool used by the owner of the ShortsGTAVI channel to publish, on the owner's own TikTok and YouTube profiles, short clips that the owner has reviewed and approved.

Privacy Policy Terms of Service

What it does

The owner reviews each short video in a private dashboard. Only approved videos are sent to the owner's own TikTok account, as a draft in the TikTok inbox or as a post, using TikTok's official Content Posting API. Original creators are always credited.
