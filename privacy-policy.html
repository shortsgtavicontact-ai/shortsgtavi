ShortsGTAVI Publisher · last updated November 19, 2026

ShortsGTAVI Publisher is a personal tool used only by the owner of the ShortsGTAVI channel. It accesses only the owner's own TikTok account, with the owner's authorization, to upload videos that the owner has reviewed and approved.

Data we use

Sharing

We do not sell, rent or share any data. Access tokens are stored only on the owner's computer.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time by removing the app in TikTok > Settings and privacy > Security > Apps and services.
